# Natural price endings

A price that ends the way local prices end reads as a real local price. One that ends like a converted price (¥731, ₹413.27) reads as an afterthought. Round each target to the market's natural ending at or just below it (`method.md`, section 4).

**This list is guidance, not fact.** Conventions shift, and stores' price grids decide what's allowed. Every run, check the ending against the anchors' and competitors' own local prices you just collected, and against the store's grid (`store-rules.md`). Where they disagree with this list, follow what you found and say so.

## How to find a market's ending

1. Look at the local prices you collected for the anchors and competitors: the most common ending among them is usually right.
2. Look at the store's price grid for that currency (some stores allow only fixed price points).
3. If both are silent, use the pattern of the nearest similar market, and mark it low confidence.

## Common patterns (verify each time)

| Market or currency | Common pattern | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| US dollar, Canadian dollar, Australian dollar, pound sterling | .99, sometimes .49 | 4.99, 2.49 |
| Euro | ,99 (some markets also ,49 or whole euros) | 4,99 €, 2,49 € |
| Swiss franc | .00 or .50, sometimes .90 | CHF 5.00, CHF 4.50 |
| Nordic kronor (SEK, NOK, DKK) | whole units ending in 9 | 49 kr, 59 kr |
| Japanese yen | whole tens, often ending in 0 (x00, x50, x80) | ¥480, ¥500, ¥1,500 |
| Korean won | steps of ₩1,100 at the low end (VAT-inclusive grids), then round thousands with 900 | ₩3,300, ₩5,500, ₩9,900 |
| Indian rupee | whole rupees ending in 9 | ₹99, ₹149, ₹199 |
| Brazilian real | ,90 (sometimes ,99) | R$ 9,90, R$ 14,90 |
| Mexican peso | whole pesos ending in 9 | $49, $99, $129 |
| Singapore dollar | .98 at the low end of store grids, otherwise .99 or .90 | S$1.98, S$4.98 |
| Indonesian rupiah | round thousands | Rp 15.000, Rp 29.000 |
| Vietnamese dong | round thousands | 25.000 ₫, 49.000 ₫ |
| Turkish lira | ,99 or whole liras ending in 9 | 49,99 ₺, 99 ₺ |
| South African rand | .99 or whole rand ending in 9 | R49.99, R59 |

## Rules of thumb

- **Below the round number, not above it:** 49 reads better than 51; but don't drop a full step only to reach a "9".
- **Whole units in low-value currencies:** no decimals where the currency's smallest coins aren't used in prices.
- **Keep products on the same pattern in one market:** a monthly at ₹149 and a yearly at ₹1,199, not ₹1,213.
- **Tax-inclusive markets** show the final price; the ending applies to the price people see, not to the price before tax.
